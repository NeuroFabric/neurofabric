Quarterly Update — Project Falcon

This quarter the team shipped the v2 onboarding flow, reducing
signup drop-off by an estimated 18%. Infrastructure costs rose
9% due to increased load testing. Two open risks remain: the
payments integration is still on a deprecated API version, and
the mobile app has not been load-tested above 500 concurrent
users. Next quarter priorities: migrate payments API, run a
mobile load test, and begin design work on the referrals feature.
