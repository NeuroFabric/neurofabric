chore(tsconfig): declare skill-registry so the registry is consumed, not `any`

G1 remainder, 3 of 9 overall. `allowJs` stays OFF, per ruling: the strict sweep
covers TypeScript only, and JavaScript is consumed through declaration files
rather than type-checked directly. platform/skill-registry.mjs had none, so
every `.ts` file importing it got TS7016 and the `.map`/`.find` callbacks over
`discoverSkills(...)` got TS7006. Six errors, one root cause, and all six clear
together or not at all.

The declaration REUSES the source of truth rather than restating it.
`discoverSkills` returns `SkillDescriptor` and `formatSkillCatalog` accepts it,
both imported from core/types.ts, so a change to the skill contract reaches
every consumer without anyone editing this file.

FINDING, surfaced by typing it: the JSDoc on `skillRiskLevel` in the .mjs
declares a THREE-value risk union ("READ_ONLY" | "CONTROLLED" | "WRITE"), but
the value it is handed is a four-value `RiskLevel` that also includes
DESTRUCTIVE, because `parseSkillDocument` builds a `SkillDescriptor`. Typing the
declaration to the JSDoc made four call sites fail to compile, which is how the
discrepancy became visible. The declaration follows the implementation
(`RiskLevel` in and out) and the JSDoc discrepancy is filed rather than
propagated. Correcting the JSDoc on the .mjs is a production edit and does not
belong in a type increment.

The other three TS2554 in these files are PRE-EXISTING and belong to G3, not
here: `recentEvents(200)` is called with an argument at three sites while
`recentEvents()` declares none. They were not introduced by this change.
